# Plates — changelog

Version-keyed, because a published site pins `template_version` and a snapshot
is never changed by improving a template. If your site is on an older version,
this is what you would get by upgrading.

## v1 — first release

Promoted from `design/gallery/comp.html` (the design record, kept in the repo).

- A catalogue of work: each project is a plate whose image frame bleeds to one
  edge, with its words in the opposite margin, alternating sides down the page.
- **Its rule: no text is ever set over an image.** Beyond the look, this is what
  keeps the contrast floor checkable — a photograph uploaded after the design
  ships has unknown tones, so text over it could not be audited.
- Shows settings, projects, experiences and testimonials. **No education** — a
  catalogue lists work, and the closing colophon lists roles rather than schools.
  Education written in the builder is kept and appears on a design that uses it.
- Options: number the plates, break the run with an approved quote, lead with a
  starred project.
- Projects with no screenshot get a deliberate empty frame rather than a broken
  one, which is what most new accounts will see.
