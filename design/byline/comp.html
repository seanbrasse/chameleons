Selected work

  1. Onboarding, rebuilt around the first ten minutes

    Cut time-to-first-value from three days to under an hour, and activation rose 34% the quarter it shipped.

    The signup flow assumed patience nobody had. I cut it to a single decision, moved verification to the background, and let people reach a real result before we asked for anything. The hard part was convincing three teams that fewer steps was not less rigor.

  2. A pricing page that finally told the truth

    Rewrote pricing end to end; support tickets about billing fell by half.

    People were not confused about the price — they were confused about what they were buying. I threw out the tier names, wrote the page like a person explaining it to a friend, and shipped it in a week against a lot of institutional nervousness.

  3. The migration nobody noticed

    Moved 40,000 accounts to a new billing system with zero customer-facing downtime.

    A year of quiet work whose entire success metric was that no one wrote in. I ran it as a series of reversible steps, each shippable on its own, so we were never one big launch away from disaster.
